Leadership Coaching

One-on-one coaching for leaders who are running on empty. Build clarity, confidence and resilience, plus habits that actually stick.

Book a free discovery call

Sound familiar?

You put everyone else first. Then something gives.

When you're leading a team, you need to be in good physical and mental health to perform at your best. But too often work, the team and the family come first, and it's not until overwhelm or burnout shows up that you realise something has to change.

That's where I come in. I mix mental fitness coaching with real-world leadership experience, to give you a supportive space to reflect, grow and build sustainable habits that lift both your performance and your wellbeing.

What's included

DISC ADVANCED® Leadership Psychometric Assessment, to review your natural strengths, challenge areas and leadership goals

Packages of 6 or 12 coaching sessions, weekly or fortnightly, to set your vision and the habits you need to get there

Support and encouragement in between sessions to keep you accountable and motivated

Proven coaching strategies to bring out the best in you and have you performing at your peak

(Leadership Plus +) A personalised coaching planner to set your vision, habits and goals

This is for you if...

  • You're feeling stuck in your job and know something needs to change.
  • You're ready to take action on the stuff that affects your wellbeing: sleep, nutrition, mental fitness and movement.
  • You want to see the link between your health and performing at your peak at work.
  • You want to understand why past attempts at change haven't stuck.

This is not for you if...

  • You want someone to tell you what to do (and what not to do).
  • You're not ready to dig into why change hasn't stuck before.
  • You can't commit 2-4 hours a month to your own growth.

Choose a plan

This isn't another thing on your to-do list to tick and flick. Habits take time, and I'm in it for the long haul if you are. Buckle up, buttercup. Pick the plan that suits you right now, and we'll decide together whether weekly or fortnightly works best.

The Bud

A refresher after 6 or 12 sessions with me. Set some new goals and get back on track.

Leadership Plus +
