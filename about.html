About Julz

Meet Julz.

Hello People Co. creates workshops for bold humans at work.

Send an emailBook a call

Done in the room, not on autopilot. Workshops that stay human in a world rushing toward templates, shortcuts and tick-box training.

I'm Julz. A leadership and team facilitator with 15+ years in People & Culture (HR), followed by 6 years running my own leadership training and coaching business, working with organisations that genuinely care about their people and the impact they have on others. Using DISC Advanced® behavioural profiling, I help teams and leaders understand how they show up, how they're experienced, and how to adapt with each other and the people they serve.

This work sits at the intersection of self-awareness, leadership and performance. It's practical, commercial, and deeply human.

Hello People Co. is based in Queensland, but the work is intentionally in-person and Australia-wide. I travel to where teams are, because real connection, honest conversations and lasting change don't happen through a screen alone.

I'm working with organisations who want to make real change. The bold and brave teams.
